## United States: ADA Title I reasonable accommodation request

[Date]

To: [HR contact / supervisor], [Organisation]
From: [Name], [Role]
Re: Request for reasonable accommodation under the Americans with Disabilities Act, Title I

I am writing to request reasonable accommodations for a neurological condition that substantially limits major life activities, including thinking and concentrating, and the neurological major bodily function (42 U.S.C. 12102(2)).

**Condition.** Developmental topographical disorientation (DTD) is a documented neurodevelopmental condition (Iaria and Barton 2010; van der Ham et al. 2025 systematic review; prevalence about 3 percent, Piccardi et al. 2022) with strong familial aggregation (Barclay et al. 2016; Burles and Iaria 2020). It has no dedicated diagnostic code; the nearest placements are ICD-11 6A0Y and DSM-5-TR other or unspecified neurodevelopmental disorder (F88/F89). It has no specific statutory recognition, so this request is framed by analogy with other cognitive impairments that substantially limit a major life activity. The accommodations requested are standard cognitive-accommodation practice and carry no cost beyond staff time.

**Functional impact.** [In my own words: ...] In practical terms: I cannot form a mental map of a place, and familiar places do not become easier with repetition. Direction words (left, right, north, south) do not reliably correspond to physical directions for me, so a spoken instruction such as "left two blocks then right" may be carried out as "right then right". I navigate reliably by landmark sequences. Under time pressure my deliberate workaround fails. [Profile flags, if any.] [Family history, if included: "Three relatives across two generations report the same lifelong difficulty; familial aggregation of DTD is documented (Barclay et al. 2016; Burles and Iaria 2020). This pedigree is my own report."]

**Accommodations requested.**
1. Written, landmark-based directions for any new site, delivered before the first visit. [Grade B]
2. A consistent workstation and meeting-room assignment; changes communicated with a photo of the door. [Grade B]
3. A buddy or escort for the first visit to any new building, client site, or venue. [Grade B]
4. Use of navigation aids during work travel, with an escort alternative where devices are not permitted. [Grade B]
5. Meeting invitations that include a photo of the room entrance and one sentence stating what I will be facing on arrival. [Grade C]
6. No performance expectation that depends on unaided wayfinding. [Grade C]
7. [Other]

I welcome the interactive process and can provide a clinical summary letter on request.

[Name]

Evidence grades: A replicated group studies or systematic review; B one large or well-designed study; C extrapolation or expert opinion; F family report only. Research note dated 2026-10-17.

## France : lettre de demande d'amenagement de poste

[Date]

A l'attention de : [responsable RH / referent handicap], [Organisation]
De : [Nom], [Poste]
Objet : demande d'amenagement raisonnable du poste de travail (Code du travail, art. L5213-6 ; loi 2005-102)

**Situation.** La desorientation topographique developpementale (DTD) est un trouble neurodeveloppemental documente (Iaria et Barton 2010 ; revue systematique de van der Ham et al. 2025 ; prevalence d'environ 3 pour cent, Piccardi et al. 2022) a forte agregation familiale (Barclay et al. 2016 ; Burles et Iaria 2020). Il ne dispose d'aucun code diagnostique specifique ; les classements les plus proches sont CIM-11 6A0Y et, dans le DSM-5-TR, le trouble neurodeveloppemental autre ou non specifie. Il ne beneficie d'aucune reconnaissance legale specifique ; la presente demande s'appuie par analogie sur le cadre general du handicap cognitif. Les amenagements demandes relevent de la pratique courante et n'engendrent aucun cout au-dela du temps de personnel. [Si RQTH obtenue : "Je suis titulaire d'une RQTH delivree le [date]."]

**Impact fonctionnel.** [Dans mes mots : ...] Concretement : je ne forme pas de carte mentale des lieux ; les termes de direction (gauche, droite, nord, sud) ne correspondent pas de maniere fiable aux directions reelles ; je m'oriente de maniere fiable par sequences de reperes ; sous pression, ma strategie de compensation echoue. [Profils.] [Antecedents familiaux.]

**Amenagements demandes.**
1. Itineraires ecrits bases sur des reperes, remis avant toute premiere visite d'un site. [Grade B]
2. Poste de travail et salle de reunion fixes ; tout changement accompagne d'une photo de la porte. [Grade B]
3. Accompagnement lors de la premiere visite d'un batiment, d'un site client ou d'un lieu d'evenement. [Grade B]
4. Autorisation des aides a la navigation en deplacement, avec accompagnement lorsque les appareils sont interdits. [Grade B]
5. Invitations de reunion comportant une photo de l'entree de la salle et une phrase indiquant ce que j'aurai en face de moi a l'arrivee. [Grade C]
6. Aucun objectif de performance dependant d'une orientation sans aide. [Grade C]

Je reste a votre disposition pour en discuter et peux fournir un bilan neuropsychologique.
